Fighter RPG Showdown 3D — Playground A

Design artifact, not the game. Four feel presets over the app's real components — flip between them, fine-tune the tokens live, then hit Copy tokens on whatever you land on.

Frames: 1920×1080 primary · 21:9 ultrawide check · TV title-safe guides. Gamepad-first — every screen carries a visible focus state and a button legend; nothing requires a cursor.
